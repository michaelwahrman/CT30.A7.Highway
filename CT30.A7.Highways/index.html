CT30.A7 · Highways

A traffic helicopter over a jammed freeway network gone mad: 348 lanes, six interchanges, express viaducts and four diagonal freeways high over everything. Everything moves smoothly: the helicopter eases into and out of every move, and lanes ease up to speed and down to a stop. Your 8 northbound lanes are bumper to bumper; nobody changes lanes, and each lane moves at its own speed (knobs 1–8). Around them: the southbound side, ramp lanes running between interchanges, three cross freeways and, 1.7 km west, a parallel freeway. Below you is suburbia (from the suburb photos); three diagonal freeways cross everything on overpasses, at angles, with no ramps. Every 3.2 km there is a freeway-to-freeway interchange — a cloverleaf (south), a six-level stack (middle) and a cloverstack (north) — and every ramp carries its own lane of jammed traffic. Next ▸ flies you to the next one. The roads are fixed; only the traffic moves. Cars are your top-down car photos (SUVs, vans, pickups, sports cars, station wagons, a sedan and an electric coupé), each repainted in a realistic colour.

MIDI

LAYER A = Next interchange, LAYER B = text. Launchpad Mini: rows 1–2 = the same 16 buttons; rows 3–8 = a speed ladder per lane (column = lane, top row 60 mph … bottom row stop). nanoKEY2: see the MIDI panel.
