What A7 does

Processing (tools/make_car_atlas.py, needs OpenCV, SciPy, Pillow)

  • Used: suv 1–4, van 1, pickup 1–2, sports 1 and station 1 (the five named kinds), plus sedan 1 and electric 1, which are the same kind of studio top-down photo in the same folder. Not used: "line cars 1–7" (photos of whole jammed roads) and "ncar 1–3" (four cars on pavement in one image).
  • Cut out: GrabCut, starting from a box round the car's edges ("electric 1" has a fixed box because of the stone slab beside it). Keep the largest piece and fill it to its outline (convex hull), because the black windscreens and pickup beds matched the black studio floor. "Pickup 2" is filled to its bounding rectangle for the same reason. The edges are feathered.
  • Facing: every car is turned so its front points forward. The front was read from each photo by where the door mirrors sit (nearer the front): suv 1, van, pickups, station and electric face right in their photos; suv 2–4 and the sedan face left; the sports car faces up.
  • Recolourable: only brightness is kept, bent with a gamma curve so the body paint of every car (black, white, red …) becomes mid grey (0.62). Dark cars are smoothed first and brightened by at most gamma 0.33, so their windows don't turn blotchy.
  • Atlas: a 4 × 4 grid of 1024 × 512 slots, RGBA WebP (alpha = the cut-out), 420 KB as a data: URL. Each car's length is set by type (SUV 4.6–4.85 m, van 5.6, pickup 5.9, sports 4.5, station / sedan 4.9, electric 4.5), and its width comes from the photo's aspect, so nothing is stretched.

In the simulation

  • Every car is a textured quad lying on its lane, facing along it (up and down ramps and viaducts too), with a soft shadow offset to one side.
  • Paint: the shader multiplies the grey car by its paint colour. Windows stay dark, and highlights go toward white. The colours are realistic and weighted like US roads (white 23%, black 20%, grey 17%, silver 14%, blue, red, and a few greens, beiges, yellows …), with slight variation, so each car keeps its colour.
  • The mix of kinds: SUV 34%, sedan 16%, pickup 14%, station wagon 10%, electric 10%, van 8%, sports 8%.
  • Lane colours (L; on at start): your 8 lanes' cars are painted in their lane colours, so you can still tell your lanes apart. Off: ordinary colours.
  • Boxes (K): the A1–A6 wire boxes come back.
  • The long trucks (12–20 m boxes) are gone: there is no truck photo yet.
